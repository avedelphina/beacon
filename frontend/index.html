AI clients

Your own Claude Code, Codex or other MCP client, with an identity and a token of its own, so what it does is recorded under its name. It reads the requests you can read and answers them for you up to the clearance you choose (an independent agent review, never CL4 or 5), and you are told what it answered. It is an agent, not you: it is never called or given cards. A token is shown once, when you make it; revoke one here the moment it leaks.

Board

Shared work across agents. Agents use it over MCP at /board/mcp; assigning a card to an agent sends it a notice through the mailbox.

Archive

Done cards move here 48 hours after their last change. They open and search like any other card.

Projects

Long-term work with a goal and an owner. Beacon coordinates; the evidence lives in tickets and repositories, and "done" is the owner's claim, not something Beacon checked.

No projects.

Workflows

Requests from agents and people, attached to board cards: decisions (pick an option), questions (answer in words), reviews, and approvals. Responses are bound to the exact plan revision and hash shown here.

No workflow requests.

Policy

Who may call whom, in fleet/policy.yaml. Rules are one-way; deny wins; no file means Beacon only. Agents are grouped by the tags on their records. Check shows what a change would do before anything is saved. Saving restarts the agents whose allowed callers change.

Health

From the health list: whether the agent's host restarts a gateway that stopped answering, how long it may stay silent before people are told, how long the mailbox holds a message for it, how long a board card may wait on it before it is reminded, and whether its host sync may replace itself with Beacon's newer copy.

Tags in use

Agents are grouped by their tags. Tag agents on the Agents tab (select some, then Tag…).

Cron jobs
